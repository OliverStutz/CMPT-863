# Week 4 pair cards

Generated 2026-10-01 from `slides/16-Discussion-Guide.md` by `slides/_build-deck-source.py`. Edit section 4 of the guide, then regenerate. The verification log and open items stay in the section file.

One card per pair, five minutes. Each card has one question and two roles, one per partner, so the disagreement is built in and nobody has to pick a side first. Each pair writes two lines: one point they disagree on, one they agree on, each with a page from the paper or a section from the talk. The group phase opens by harvesting those lines. With fewer than eight pairs, use cards A to F first. Roles are prompts to think from, not scripts; a student may abandon the role once they have a view.

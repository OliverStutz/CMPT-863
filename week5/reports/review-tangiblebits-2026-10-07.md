# Review of tangiblebits.md (Tangible Bits, Week 5), 2026-10-07

Method: draft written from the PDF text of "Week 5 - Tangible Bits-1.pdf" (week5 folder, printed pp. 234-241), then checked by me and two independent Opus reviewers with the same brief (rules taken from review-commentary-2026-10-07.md, the Zooids review). Vote counts are out of 3. All items marked applied were fixed in tangiblebits.md the same day; the first draft is not kept.

## Must fix (applied)

1. Competing superlatives (3 of 3): "The claim I most want tested", "The design decision I would steal first", "the one I keep returning to". Now "The claim I want tested", "One design decision I would steal is small", "draws the clearest line in the paper".
2. Invented quotation (3 of 3): "informs without distracting" was in quotation marks but is not in the paper. Quotes removed, sentence rewritten.
3. "The one observation that is reported" (2 of 3): the paper also reports finding the display compelling and the water tank being received enthusiastically. Now "The only problem the authors report cuts the other way: they found the display compelling, but ...".
4. Remedy incomplete (3 of 3): rain vs ripples cannot show "without distracting" without a baseline. Added a no-display condition, and the time from noticing to the detail view on the desk as a fourth measure (the hand-off is part of the authors' stated key challenge).
5. Internal contradiction in Like 2 (2 of 3): the paragraph opened with two physical phicons whose rotation was ambiguous, then claimed that with physical objects "expectation and behaviour cannot drift apart". Now "the designer can build the expectation into the object". The semantic-light flashlight, which deliberately breaks optical physics, was dropped from the paragraph.
6. Attributed link (2 of 3): "The discussion section turns this into a principle" implied the paper connects the rotation instrument to the optical metaphors; it does not. Now "I read the discussion of optical metaphors as the same principle". "So the activeLENS is modelled on ..." reversed the paper's order (the lens came first) and was cut.
7. Zooids agency (1 of 3, but matches last week's fix): "a phicon that can move itself" became "a phicon the computer can drive", since Zooids are centrally coordinated.

## Factual slips (applied)

- "both a container of bits" is not contiguous; paper has "acts both as a container of bits ... and as a handle for manipulating the map". Quotes corrected.
- "a toy train": paper says "imaginary toy train".
- "let a two-year-old know his mother was busy": the paper dates the first meeting with the abacus to age two, not the accounting anecdote. Now "the young Ishii".
- "move to the center only when something changes": the paper gives two triggers (anomaly, or a lull in the foreground). "only" dropped.
- "the walk to the desk not described as built" was an inference; now "the paper does not say whether the hand-off to the desk was built".
- "The ambition stated almost in passing": it is the paper's stated ultimate goal. Sentence cut with the Radical Atoms lineage (already in the Zooids block).
- "rather than guessing in software": now "rather than pick an interpretation in software"; "built" became "designed", the paper's word.
- "had to elicit a gesture set to match that expectation" overstated Wobbrock's motive; now "elicited a gesture set from users because designers could not predict that expectation".
- "ripples are projected on the ceiling": the light is reflected off the water; now "reflected onto the ceiling".
- Summary: "No prototype is evaluated" became "no formal evaluation; informal observations only"; the Augmented Reality entry now contrasts with the common visual-overlay approach, not DigitalDesk specifically.
- Notes: connections line corrected.

## Fit and form (applied)

- Length: 931 words with eleven sentences over 40 words, against 729 for the Zooids block. Now 790 words, 6 sentences per paragraph, longest sentence 42 words.
- Like 1 was mostly a retelling of the Geospace demo (both reviewers). The demo is now one sentence; the freed space carries the point that a phicon trades the mouse's generality for specificity, and that Zooids recovers some of it.
- Like 2 carried four threads (instrument, optical principle, Wobbrock, Zooids LEDs) plus a closing aphorism about touch screens. Now one spine (build the expectation into the object) with the instrument and Wobbrock as evidence. The Zooids LED sentence was cut because it repeats the Zooids block almost word for word.
- Like 3 carried three points (Weiser, framing images, states of matter). Now the Weiser contrast with the abacus as its illustration; the states-of-matter sentence is cut.
- Rating consistency with Zooids (2 of 3): both reviewers gave 5/5 but asked for one clause explaining why a missing study does not cost a vision paper the point it cost Zooids. Added to the Wish: "A vision paper is entitled to leave this open".
- Wish position: last week's review said last, the template and the accepted Zooids block say first. Kept first; noted in the file.

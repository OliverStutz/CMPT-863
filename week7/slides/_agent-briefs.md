**Page 937 (PDF 1).** Abstract: research through design "is likely to produce theories that are provisional, contingent, and aspirational"; "this variation need not be seen as a sign of inadequate standards or a lack of cumulative progress in the field, but may be natural for a generative endeavour"; "theory as annotation of realised design examples, and particularly portfolios of related pieces"; "the design research community should be wary of impulses towards convergence and standardisation." The opponents, as Gaver quotes them: research through design "'lacks clear expectations and standards for what constitutes "good" design research'" and would benefit from "'some actionable metrics for bringing rigor in critique of design research'" ([9] p824, the CHI 2011 panel Forlizzi, DiSalvo, Bardzell, Koskinen, Wensveen); the community should "'develop protocols, descriptions, and guidelines for its processes, procedures, and activities'" in ways assumed to be "'like any other research approach'" ([35] p317, Zimmerman, Stolterman, Forlizzi DIS 2010); "'a proper research methodology that can produce relevant and rigorous theory'" ([35] p316); conceptual contributions should exhibit "'a level of extensibility and verifiability'" to comprise "'theory designers can apply in research in practice'" ([33], Zimmerman and Forlizzi 2008). Nonetheless, I am uneasy about some of the tendencies that seem to underlie both the diagnoses and the treatments that have been suggested." Standards "may be a route towards disciplinary legitimacy within HCI"; the risk is "a form of self-policing that would be overly restrictive of a form of research that I" (continues p. 938) "value for its ability to continually and creatively challenge status quo thinking."

## 1.5 Style rules

- **No em-dashes anywhere** (the character U+2014). Do not use en-dashes (U+2013) as dashes either. Use a comma, a full stop, or "to" for ranges ("pp. 937 to 946"). Hyphens in compound words are fine. When a quotation from the paper contains a dash, either stop the quotation before the dash, or replace the dashed clause with "..." and note the omission in the log. Never reproduce the dash.
- The paper uses single quotation marks for its own quotations. In our files, use double quotation marks for anything quoted from the paper, and keep the paper's inner single marks as they are.

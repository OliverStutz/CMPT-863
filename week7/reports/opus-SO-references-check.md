## Should fix

1. **Slide 9, card A (attribution).** "must founder" is Gaver's own sentence (p. 942: "Pre-paradigmatic research, in contrast, must founder."), stronger than Kuhn's wording; slides2/04-Puzzle.md says to present it "in Gaver's words." With "(Kuhn)" right after it, the card reads as a Kuhn quotation.
   Current: `- **A. Baby science.** No shared paradigm yet; such research "must founder." (Kuhn)`
   Replace: `- **A. Baby science.** No shared paradigm yet; in Gaver's words, such research "must founder." (after Kuhn)`
2. **Slide 9, card B (attribution).** "most of us" is Gaver speaking of design researchers (p. 942), not Latour. The tag "(Latour)" right after it reads as Latour's phrase.
   Current: `- **B. Messier up close.** Science may be just as contested day to day; "most of us" seem to share core values. (Latour)`
   Replace: `- **B. Messier up close.** Science may be just as contested day to day (Latour); and, Gaver says, "most of us" seem to share core values.`
3. **Slide 14, line 2 (attribution).** "caption" is the presenter's word; slides2/05-Rams.md Watch out says so and notes that Gaver's word is "annotation" and that he insists "neither dominates, and neither is subservient" (p. 945). Under the label "Gaver's order" it reads as his.
   Current: `- Gaver's order: the artefacts are the "definite facts." Theory is the caption.`
   Replace: `- Gaver's order: the artefacts are the "definite facts." Theory is the annotation (our word: the caption).`
   (If the title "theory is the caption" stays, the replacement line is enough to mark the word as ours.)
4. **Slide 19, row 2 (subject of the quotation).** The paper says "attempts to establish disciplinary norms of process or outcome are political acts to be approached with care" (p. 945). The slide makes the norms themselves the political acts.
   Current: `- 2. Disciplinary norms "are political acts to be approached with care"`
   Replace: `- 2. Attempts to set disciplinary norms "are political acts to be approached with care"`
5. **Slide 12, image credit.** The products-only crop keeps the photo credit numbers (1 to 4) but the slide carries no credit line; the Flickr credit appears only on slide 13.
   Add a line: `- Images: Flickr Creative Commons (Nick Wade, Rene Spitz, Brett Wayn, '•'), from Figure 1 of the paper.`

7. Slide 2 credit: the log entry is CHI 2004 Extended Abstracts; "CHI 2004 EA" would be more exact. "Porthole" is the notes' description of the photograph, not a source term; it matches Figure 1.
8. Slide 21: the Drift Table image includes the printed caption "Figure 1. The Drift Table."; slide 2 asks for it to be cropped, slide 21 does not. Use the same crop.
9. Slide 1: the strip from the annotated Rams figure carries no credit; add "Figure 1, Gaver 2012" in small type or rely on slide 13.
10. Slide 16: "The critics' way" is the presenter's lane label (slides2/06-Aftermath.md Watch out); Höök and Löwgren 2012 cite the essay. An "(our lanes)" tag would mirror the other "ours" labels.
11. Slide 20, row 1: "Productive" is capitalised inside the quotation; the paper has "productive research programmes" (p. 941).

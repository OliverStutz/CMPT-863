# Slide 1 raw output
Image: s1.png

1. **Budget figure has no breakdown or timeframe**
   Detail: The slide asks for approval of $2.4 million but does not say what it covers, over what period, or whether it is capital or recurring spending. A board would usually expect at least a top-level split before approving.
   Region: Right-hand callout, "$2.4 million: budget request for approval today".

2. **Single shared network flagged without stated risk**
   Detail: All 12 warehouses, head office, scanners, trucks and payroll run on one network, which suggests a flat design where one breach reaches everything. The slide does not state this risk or what the request would change about it.
   Region: First bullet, "Our 12 warehouses and head office run on one network: orders, scanners, trucks and payroll".

3. **"Attackers are getting better" is unsupported**
   Detail: The claim is general and gives no evidence, source or example specific to the company or its sector. A board would usually expect a reference point such as an industry report or incident trend.
   Region: Third bullet, "Attackers are getting better".

4. **Break-in attempts lack detail on impact**
   Detail: The slide says two attempted break-ins were stopped but gives no indication of how they were detected, how close they came, or what they would have cost. Without this the board cannot judge how urgent the request is.
   Region: Second bullet, "This year we stopped two attempted break-ins into that network".

5. **Insurer and customer expectations not specified**
   Detail: The slide does not say what the insurer or the largest customer actually require, by when, or what happens if the requirement is not met (for example premium change or contract risk). These are the kinds of consequences a board would want quantified.
   Region: Fourth bullet, "Our insurer and our largest customer now expect stronger protection".

6. **No options, alternatives or cost of inaction shown**
   Detail: The slide moves straight from situation to a single budget figure without showing alternatives, a smaller option, or the expected loss if nothing is done. Boards usually expect this before an approval decision.
   Region: Whole slide, relationship between the bullets and the callout.

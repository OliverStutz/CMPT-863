# Slide 3 raw output
Image: s3.png

1. **Head office is a single point of failure**
   Detail: All 12 warehouses depend on one data centre and one internet connection, both at head office, with no second link or backup site shown. If head office is hit by an attack, power loss or line outage, every site stops at once, which is the outcome the previous risk framing warns about.
   Region: Diagram centre, "Head office / Main data centre", and bullets "All 12 warehouses connect to our data centre at head office" and "One internet connection, at head office, watched day and night".

2. **No backups or recovery plan shown**
   Detail: The design covers keeping attackers out but shows nothing about backups, offline copies or how quickly systems would be restored after an attack. Recovery capability is usually what most reduces outage length and is a standard board expectation.
   Region: Whole slide; absent from both the diagram and the bullet list.

3. **Remote staff line bypasses the internet**
   Detail: The dashed line runs from remote staff directly to head office, but staff at home or on the road would in practice connect through the internet. The diagram therefore misrepresents where remote access enters the network.
   Region: Bottom of diagram, dashed line from "Remote staff" laptop to "Head office".

4. **Perimeter-only approach misses common controls**
   Detail: The plan relies on firewalls and one monitored connection, but common practice also includes measures such as multi-factor sign-in, protection on individual computers, and staff training against phishing. Their absence makes the design look narrower than typical current practice.
   Region: Right-hand bullets, especially "A firewall at every site keeps warehouse machines apart from office computers".

5. **"Watched day and night" has no owner stated**
   Detail: The slide does not say who does the round-the-clock monitoring (in-house team or outside provider) or what happens when something is detected. For a 24/7 claim a board would usually expect this.
   Region: Bullet, "One internet connection, at head office, watched day and night".

6. **Firewall separation not shown in diagram**
   Detail: The bullet says each site firewall keeps warehouse machines apart from office computers, but the diagram shows each warehouse only as a single box with a shield, so the internal separation cannot be seen.
   Region: Warehouse boxes with shield icons, and first bullet.

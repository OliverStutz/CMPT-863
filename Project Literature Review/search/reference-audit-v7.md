## 4. Exact fixes to apply, ranked

1. **C1, SEC clause (wrong).** In paragraph 1 replace "made such oversight mandatory for listed companies, which must describe how the board oversees risks from cybersecurity threats" with "made it mandatory for U.S. public companies to describe how the board oversees risks from cybersecurity threats". Basis: release p. 60 ("to inform investors, not to influence whether and how companies manage their cybersecurity risk") and p. 1 ("public companies that are subject to the reporting requirements of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934"). The working note's "SEC ... made such oversight mandatory" should be corrected the same way.
2. **C19, evidence type.** Replace "All of this evidence comes from interviews" with "All of this evidence comes from interviews and surveys" (Lowry p. 796 is a survey of 33 CISOs, cited in the same paragraph).
3. **C7, Opdenbusch budget claim.** Replace "and that the budget is the only cybersecurity decision most boards make." with "and that, by most participants' account, the budget is the only cybersecurity decision their boards make (p. 5)."
4. **C12, Opdenbusch locator.** Replace "(p. 2) because they decide what reaches the board" with "(p. 2) because they decide what reaches the board (p. 11)".
5. **C4, Lowry attribution.** Replace "whereas expert directors and executives judged that they could not interpret the answers" with "whereas expert directors and consultants judged that they could not interpret the answers (pp. 792–793)".
6. **C11, Microsoft wording.** Replace "consumer tools already answer" with "commercial AI tools already answer", and "and suggest, during a meeting, where the holes in a participant's argument lie" with "and, during a meeting, answer a user's question about where the holes in a participant's argument lie".

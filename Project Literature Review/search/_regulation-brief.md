## Why

Section 1 of the literature review ("Cybersecurity oversight by non-expert boards") opens with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's 2023 rule: listed companies must describe the board's oversight of cybersecurity risk (Item 106(c)), and the SEC withdrew its proposal that companies disclose whether any director has cybersecurity expertise (Item 407(j)), reasoning that directors with broad-based skills "often effectively oversee management's efforts without specific subject matter expertise" (p. 85 of Release 33-11216). Oliver asked why a U.S. regulator is the anchor and wants the regulatory basis researched properly. The project is at SFU (Canada); the study is an online experiment with a board member who holds a VLM content checker during a CISO's briefing.

## Fixed questions (answer each; the three reviews will be tallied)

Q1. Does the proposal meet every requirement in Instructions.md? Answer "yes", "with changes" or "no", then list each requirement with met / not met and why.
Q2. For each subsection 1 to 5: is the bundle coherent, meaning every paper in it answers the subsection's question? "yes" or "no" per subsection, with the papers that do not fit.
Q3. Moves: list every paper you would move, as "paper: from section X to section Y, because ...". "None" is a valid answer.
Q4. Alternative bundlings: for Alt A, Alt B and Alt C in the proposal, "adopt" or "reject", one sentence each.
Q5. Optional sources (Da Silva & Jensen 2022; Shaw et al. 2003; Sharma et al. 2023; Pezzo et al. 2022; Hancock et al. 2020): for each, "include", "drop" or "only if words allow", with the section it would serve.
Q6. Is any source in the pool surplus for a 1,500-word review (would be cited without doing work)? Name it and say why. "None" allowed.
Q7. Is any theme missing that the four instruction questions (what exists, what we know, what is unresolved, how we differ) need and no subsection can answer with its bundle? Name it and the sources that would fill it, from the pool or the results files.
Q8. Word budget: realistic or not; give your own budget per section summing to at most 1,500.
Q9. Order of the subsections: keep or reorder, one line why. And any author decision you think harms the review, stated plainly.
Q10. Confidence in your answers: low, medium, high.

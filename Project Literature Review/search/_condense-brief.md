# Brief: condense the literature review

Written 2026-10-19. The instructions allow 1,000 to 1,500 words and say CHI related-work sections are often about 1,000. Draft v2 has 1,448 body words and over-explains in places. Two condensing passes by different agents, then checks.

## Target

Body (opening plus the five sections, without title, headings, references and notes) between 1,100 and 1,200 words after pass 1, and 1,000 to 1,150 after pass 2 if that can be done without loss. Shorter is better only when nothing the reader needs is lost.

## What to cut

- Detail that does not change what the reader concludes: second examples where one carries the point, sample sizes that are not benchmarks, restatements of a claim already made, scaffolding phrases ("in the authors' words", "in exchange experiments"), and quotations where a plain paraphrase is as exact.
- Sentences that list a paper without using it. A source may be dropped if its only job was to be present; keep at least 15 sources, and keep every source that carries a claim the structure needs (`_review-design-v2.md`, hypothesis map).
- Repetition between the "how we differ" lines of the sections and the gap section; the gap section states the combination once, in full; the sections say in one clause what they add.

## What must stay

- Five sections in the same order; the opening; the gap section ending on the contribution sentence.
- Every claim that stays must keep the wording that the checkers verified (see `check-tally.md` and `fact-check-2026-10-19.md`): Kempt and Nagel for the burden of proof; Madanay "rated lowest of the four conditions"; Squash 14 relevant and 20 irrelevant; D. Kim et al. (2026) as the bound on the AI-penalty claim and as the closest design; Du's extra drop with red highlighting not claimed on the 10-point scale; Aamir "felt more confident"; Sharma's "further development of trust" and the 8 of 94 figure; Pezzo in one cautious sentence at most; Kempt and Nagel's "reasons cannot settle it", with "a reason meets the burden" as our hypothesis.
- The author's rules: no em-dashes; plain English; the one sentence that politician results are not carried over; trust in the AI not an outcome; Gupta only in the gap; Dobbs absent; CISO and METI spelled out at first use; no hypothesis labels (H1 to H4) in the text.
- APA in-text citations and a reference list that matches the citations exactly, in the style of Abstract v8. Remove the entries of any source you drop.
- No new claims, no new sources, no number not already in v2.

## Output layout

Title; the five sections; `## References`; `## Condensing notes`: body word count by section and total, sources dropped with the reason, and any cut you considered but did not make because it would lose something, with the reason. No em-dashes anywhere in the file.

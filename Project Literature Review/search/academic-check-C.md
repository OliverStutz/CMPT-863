## 4. Register findings

- R1 (missing hedge). "The costs fall on the person checked." This is unhedged, and the paragraph itself reports a no-penalty condition (Madanay). Replace with "These checks can also impose costs on the person checked."
- R2 (overgeneralization). "Across domains, people follow AI warnings even when the warnings are wrong." The two studies that directly support the sentence both use news items. Replace with "In several settings, people follow AI warnings even when the warnings are wrong."
- R3 (APA 7 locator placement). In "'further development of trust' rather than repair ('What Are the Limits of Trust Repair?' section)", APA places the locator right after the quotation. Replace with "'further development of trust' ('What Are the Limits of Trust Repair?' section) rather than repair,".
- R4 (spelling). APA 7 uses American spelling, and the reference list already does ("behavior"). In the heading, "Effects of erroneous flags on judgement and on the person flagged" becomes "Effects of erroneous flags on judgment and on the person flagged". In the Mayer paragraph, "laypeople's judgements of an expert" becomes "laypeople's judgments of an expert".
- R5 (unclear antecedent). In "The present study combines them:", "them" has no clear referent in the preceding sentence (Sharma's design point). Replace with "The present study brings these elements together:".
- R6 (loose apposition). "and it leads to risk taking, here the budget decision" becomes "and it leads to risk taking, which in this setting is the budget decision".
- Open item (APA 7, no exact fix possible). The Copilot prompt "Where are some holes in [a meeting participant's] argument?" (Microsoft, n.d.) is a direct quotation with no locator. APA 7 asks for a paragraph number or section heading for unpaginated web sources. v5 has the same gap, and the support page is not in `references/`, so I cannot give a verified heading or paragraph number. Add "(Microsoft, n.d., para. N)" or "(Microsoft, n.d., [heading] section)" once the page is checked.
- Tense: consistent. Findings are in the past tense (found, reduced, rated, restored). Authors' arguments and conclusions are in the present (conclude, trace, argue, term, note). The present study is in the present. No change needed.
- Headings: all five are noun phrases. Abbreviations: VLM, CISO and METI are introduced at first use. SEC is never used as an abbreviation, so it needs no introduction. The citation forms "D. Kim et al." and "P. H. Kim et al." (same surname, different first authors) and "Danry et al.'s (2020)" are correct APA 7.
